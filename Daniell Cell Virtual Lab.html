Aim & theory

To set up a Daniell cell and study how concentration and temperature affect its EMF.

Anode (oxidation): Zn(s) → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻   E° = +0.76 V
Cathode (reduction): Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu(s)   E° = +0.34 V
Overall: Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu   E°cell = 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V
E = E° − (RT/nF) ln([Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺])  (n = 2)

Key terms

PartRole
Zinc electrodeAnode (−): loses mass as Zn dissolves.
Copper electrodeCathode (+): gains mass as Cu deposits.
Salt bridgeCompletes the circuit and keeps both solutions electrically neutral.
External wireElectrons flow from Zn to Cu; conventional current flows Cu to Zn.
